## Record 26 --- Campus Safety and Emergency Procedures

-   **General emergency:** Stay calm, follow authorized school
    personnel, avoid panic, move to a safe area when instructed, and
    contact emergency responders as needed.
-   **Emergency contact:** 911 in the Philippines where available;
    obtain current local fire, police, MDRRMO, and school administration
    contacts.
-   **Fire:** Raise the alarm, notify personnel, evacuate by the nearest
    safe exit, do not use elevators, and do not re-enter until
    authorities declare it safe. Use an extinguisher only if trained and
    safe.
-   **Earthquake:** Drop, Cover, and Hold On; avoid glass/falling
    objects; after shaking, evacuate if instructed or if the building is
    unsafe; watch for damage and aftershocks.
-   **Medical emergency:** Notify clinic/first aid; call responders for
    serious emergencies; do not move a seriously injured person unless
    there is immediate danger.
-   **Evacuation:** Follow posted maps/signs and staff instructions;
    keep exits clear. Routes are unconfirmed.
-   **Assembly:** Go to the designated area, stay with the group, and
    await instructions. Locations are unconfirmed.
-   **Security:** Follow entry procedures, identify yourself when
    required, and report suspicious activity/unauthorized access.
-   **Incident reporting:** Instructor, security, clinic, or responsible
    office.
-   **Possible equipment:** Extinguishers, first-aid supplies, alarms,
    emergency lights, and maps; actual locations are unknown.
-   **Contacts:** Administration, safety officer, security, clinic, and
    emergency response team.
-   **Evidence/source named:** Campus Safety Plan; actual plan was not
    supplied.
-   **Verification:** Pending confirmation.
-   **Needs confirmation:** Current emergency contacts, evacuation
    routes/assembly areas, equipment locations, designated officer,
