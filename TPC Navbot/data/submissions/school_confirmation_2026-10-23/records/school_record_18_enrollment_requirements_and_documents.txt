## Record 18 --- Enrollment Requirements and Documents

-   **New students:** Accomplished enrollment form; birth certificate;
    report card or transcript of records; good moral character
    certificate; valid ID.
-   **Returning students:** Student ID; previous academic records;
    accomplished enrollment form; required clearance or other documents
    requested by the college.
-   **Transferees:** Transcript of records/grades; honorable dismissal
    or transfer credentials; birth certificate; good moral character
    certificate; valid ID; accomplished admission/enrollment form.
-   **Shifters:** Shifting form; previous academic records; student ID;
    approval from the appropriate department/office.
-   **Number of copies:** Depends on current guidelines; confirm with
    Registrar.
-   **Accepted formats:** Originals were reported; photocopy/digital
    acceptance is not confirmed.
-   **Submission:** Registrar's Office.
-   **Deadline:** Within the official enrollment period.
-   **Missing documents:** Students may be asked to submit them before
    enrollment is finalized; conditional enrollment depends on policy.
-   **Fees:** Transcripts, certifications, and transfer credentials may
    incur fees; confirm with Registrar/Accounting.
-   **Evidence/source named:** Registrar; no actual
    confirmation/document/date was supplied.
-   **Verification:** Pending confirmation.
-   **Needs confirmation:** Official checklist per student category,
    copy counts, accepted formats, deadlines, incomplete-document
