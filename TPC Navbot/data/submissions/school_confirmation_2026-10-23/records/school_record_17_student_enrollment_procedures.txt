## Record 17 --- Student Enrollment Procedures

-   **Enrollment period:** Before each semester, according to the
    official academic calendar.
-   **Responsible office:** Registrar's Office.
-   **Location:** Main building, TPC campus.
-   **Hours:** Regular office hours on official working days; exact
    hours were not supplied in this record.
-   **Eligibility:** Qualified new, returning, transferee, and shifter
    students who meet admission requirements.
-   **General requirements reported:** Accomplished enrollment form,
    birth certificate, report card or transcript of records, good moral
    character certificate, and valid identification.
-   **General steps:** Obtain and complete the enrollment form; submit
    required documents; undergo evaluation/verification; select/register
    subjects; complete payments or clearance as applicable; confirm
    enrollment with the Registrar.
-   **New students:** Apply for admission, submit documents, undergo
    evaluation, and complete enrollment.
-   **Returning students:** Present records, settle pending
    academic/administrative requirements, and register subjects.
-   **Transferees/shifters:** Submit academic records, undergo
    evaluation, obtain approval if necessary, and complete registration.
-   **Fees:** Depend on college/program policy; confirm with Registrar
    or Accounting.
-   **Late enrollment:** May require approval and is subject to
    deadlines/policies.
-   **Evidence/source named:** Registrar; no actual
    confirmation/document/date was supplied.
-   **Verification:** Pending confirmation.
-   **Needs confirmation:** Current calendar/dates, office hours and
    room, category-specific checklists, fees, deadlines, late enrollment
    policy, and source/date.
