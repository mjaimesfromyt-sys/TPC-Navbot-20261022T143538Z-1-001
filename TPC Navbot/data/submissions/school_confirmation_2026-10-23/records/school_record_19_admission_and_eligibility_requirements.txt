## Record 19 --- Admission and Eligibility Requirements

-   **Admission:** Reported as available.
-   **Programs:** Current list depends on offerings and period; contact
    Admissions/Registrar.
-   **Eligibility:** Meet admission requirements, submit documents, and
    satisfy academic qualifications for the chosen program.
-   **Age requirements:** Must be confirmed with the college; may vary.
-   **Incoming college students:** Completion of senior high school or
    an accepted equivalent.
-   **Transferees:** Previous college academic records and transfer
    requirements; additional program qualifications may apply.
-   **Documents reported:** Application/enrollment form; birth
    certificate; senior high school report card or transcript of
    records; good moral character certificate; valid ID; other required
    documents.
-   **Process:** Inquire about programs; obtain and complete form;
    submit documents; take an entrance exam or interview if required;
    await evaluation/result; follow enrollment instructions if accepted.
-   **Entrance exam/interview:** User reports both are required, but
    whether this applies to all applicants is unconfirmed.
-   **Deadlines:** Depend on academic calendar/admission announcements.
-   **Transferees:** May need transcript of records, honorable
    dismissal/transfer credentials, and other documents for
    evaluation/approval.
-   **Where to inquire:** Visit TPC and ask the Registrar or admissions
    office.
-   **Evidence/source named:** Registrar; no actual
    confirmation/document/date was supplied.
-   **Verification:** Pending confirmation.
-   **Needs confirmation:** Current programs, exam/interview
    requirements, age/program-specific rules, dates/location/contact,
    official checklist, and source/date.
