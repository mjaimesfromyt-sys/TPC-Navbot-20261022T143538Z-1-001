## Record 14 --- Computer Laboratory Rules and Access

-   **Labs:** Computer Laboratory 1 and Computer Laboratory 2.
-   **Location:** Right-hand side when facing the flagpole from the main
    building.
-   **Schedule/access:** Reported as available during class only, for
    students in scheduled classes.
-   **Booking:** User reports no booking.
-   **Reported rules:**
    1.  No eating or drinking inside the computer lab.
    2.  Keep the lab clean and organized.
    3.  Handle computers and equipment carefully.
    4.  Do not install software without permission.
    5.  Do not change computer settings without authorization.
    6.  Use computers for academic purposes only.
    7.  Do not access inappropriate websites or content.
    8.  Do not download unauthorized files or applications.
    9.  Do not bring unauthorized USB drives or external devices.
    10. Report damaged equipment or technical problems to the lab
        instructor.
    11. Do not unplug cables or disconnect equipment.
    12. Keep noise minimal.
    13. Log out of accounts after use.
    14. Save work before shutting down.
    15. Shut down computers properly after use.
    16. Follow the instructor's instructions.
    17. Do not share passwords or personal account information.
    18. Do not handle liquids near computer equipment (overlaps with the
        no-food/drink rule).
    19. Return chairs, keyboards, and other equipment to their proper
        places.
    20. Follow lab schedules and use computers only when authorized.

    confirmation/document/date was supplied.
